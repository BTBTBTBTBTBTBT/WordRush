Edit Profile, the mascot maker and featured titles

Design options for the founder's 10-05 notes: the mascot maker is hidden behind the pencil, the featured-title list is a long scroll, and Edit Profile needs to look better. Nothing here is built. ChatGPT mockups are illustrative. Phones marked "real assets" are working HTML that uses the app's own mascot renders, lettering and badge art, so they show what can actually be built. Tap them.

1 · Audit2 · Edit Profile3 · Featured title4 · Getting players to the maker
Recommendation: make your mascot the hero of Edit Profile (A · The Stage), with a big "Make your mascot" button that opens a full-screen Dressing Room (B) where the stage stays pinned while you pick parts. Replace the title chip wall with the Title Shelves picker, which shows a live name plate. Then open the maker from four places: tapping your own avatar anywhere, a "Make me yours!" Home host, a one-time nudge after your first win, and parts you earn from achievements.

1 · Audit of today's page (iOS 2.7, demo account)

Screenshots from the iOS Simulator using the signed-in -storeDemo player WordWiz. Scroll sideways.

Stats card. The pencil (top right) is the only way in. Tapping the avatar does nothing.
Edit Profile, top. The mascot appears twice: a 64 pt preview and a 180 pt stage. The pink button between them says CHANGE PHOTO.
The maker. It starts about half a screen down. Hats, Extras, Backdrop and Frame are off the right edge of the tab row.
Picking a hat. The stage has scrolled away, so you choose from 48 gold-framed thumbnails without seeing your mascot.
Sections. There are two SAVE buttons. Seven bordered cards follow. The featured-title empty state is one line of plain text.
Bottom. Six tall social fields (about 40% of a screen), a generic globe icon, and a leaning C at the end.
Home host. Your mascot already hosts Home when it's customized. Players without a custom look see W instead, and nothing invites them to make one.

Hidden

  • The only door to the maker is an 18 pt pencil glyph next to Share on the Stats card. Web and Android use a round candy pencil, the same idea. Nothing outside Edit Profile mentions a mascot.
  • Tapping your own avatar on the Stats card, the podium, leaderboard rows or Friends does nothing.
  • Inside, the maker starts below the fold. It's preceded by a preview card and a "Change Photo" button, which pushes photos ahead of mascots.
  • 4 of the 11 category tabs (Hats, Extras, Backdrop, Frame) are off-screen. Hats is the most fun tab and is never seen first.
  • Featured titles: the demo has none, so the section is one plain sentence. That's an unfinished state under the no-placeholder rule.

Weak

  • The stage scrolls away once you open a tab. You choose hats from small thumbnails and never see the hop on your real mascot.
  • Every option tile repeats the current gold frame and backdrop, which puts about 48 gold frames on screen. The frame is louder than the hat you're choosing.
  • Two SAVE buttons, one in the header and one in the builder, with no clue which saves what.
  • The mascot stands still. It doesn't idle, blink, or react to a tap. The cast rigs already do all three.
  • Accent color only tints your username, yet takes a full card. It overlaps with mascot color and backdrop.

Bloated

  • Seven bordered section cards (Username, Bio, Accent, Title, Favorite, Privacy, Socials), each with a colored top bar. That's against the no-bordered-boxes and compact rules.
  • Socials are six always-open text fields, the tallest block on the page, for something few players fill in.
  • Title chips: the catalog has __NCAT__ visible achievements, and names run up to 27 characters ("Pure Crosswordocious Master"). An active player gets a wall of near-identical star chips in no order, with no icon, no grouping and no preview.
  • Privacy is a full card plus two lines of helper text for a single toggle.

Off-brand

  • The plain SF/Lucide pencil, the globe, the generic star.fill on every title chip, and the plain-text empty state. The app already has 117 glossy badge artworks (art-ach-*) that titles could wear.
  • The title shows as a small star pill. It should read as a ribbon that belongs to the name plate.
  • The leaning C at the bottom is decoration on a dead end. The cast belongs where it does a job.
  • Spotted on the side: the scarf covers the letter on the Chunky body (see the orange render in §4, idea 4). That breaks the mascot-maker rule against covering the face or letter.

Your mascot is the page. The top half is a stage with your real mascot at about 180 pt, standing on your chosen backdrop without a frame. It breathes and blinks, and a tap makes it hop and grin (the cast-rig idea, done with the maker's own layers). The duplicate 64 pt preview card goes away.

The maker gets its own room. MAKE YOUR MASCOT (from A, or any of the doors in §4) opens this full screen. The stage stays pinned while you scroll options, and every tap shows on your real mascot right away. This fixes the scroll-away problem.

  • Option tiles show only the part (the hat itself on a soft circle), not your whole mascot in a gold frame. That's calmer and easier to scan.
  • Category tabs are small icons drawn from the parts themselves. All 8 fit, with no hidden overflow. Pattern, Nose and Cheeks join Eyes and Mouth under a "Face" tab.
  • Randomize, Undo and Photo sit in a quiet column on the stage, so the old bottom Randomize/Save pair goes and only one SAVE remains.
  • NEW and PRO tags live on the tiles. NEW feeds the "new parts" door in §4.
  • Onboarding already uses this builder (MascotBuilderMode.onboarding), so the new room upgrades that flow too.

Effort M per platform: re-host MascotBuilderView in a full-screen sheet with a sticky stage, and change the tile renderer to show the part only.

3 · Featured title picker

Today's list is one flat grid of every unlocked title, each a star chip. Both options below wear the app's real badge art and preview the title on your name plate live. Long names (up to 27 characters) shrink to fit the ribbon instead of wrapping.

Grouped, visual, and you see it on you.

  • A live name plate at the top shows your mascot, name and the ribbon. Tapping a badge tries it on right away, and Done keeps it.
  • Recently earned comes first, since it's what you most likely want to show off.
  • Shelves follow the catalog's categories (Skill, Streaks, Firsts, Puzzles, VS, Friends…) and each shows a count such as "12 / 94". Every shelf scrolls sideways, so the sheet stays about one screen tall no matter how many titles you own.
  • Locked titles show dimmed with a lock. Tapping one shows how to earn it ("Win 30 days in a row"), which turns the picker into a goal list.
  • Search filters across every shelf.
  • It opens from the ribbon on the name plate (A), from the Title row, and from the "Achievement unlocked!" moment ("Wear it").

Effort S–M: uses the existing catalog (/api/achievements category + icon), unlocked keys and art-ach-* art. 65 of the 181 visible titles have no badge art yet, so they fall back to their category's art. One new sheet on each platform.

About the images. ChatGPT mockups (free ChatGPT, founder's browser, 10-05) are illustrative: the cast drawn there and the title names and icons are approximations, and the shipped app always uses real renders. Everything in the "real assets" phones is real: mascot renders come from packages/core avatarLayout through docs/design/brand/avatar/avatar_draw.py (the apps' own recipe), the blink frame is the real "happy" eyes part, and the lettering, badges, frames and parts come from apps/web/public/art. Titles come from the live /api/achievements catalog. Each pick ships on iOS, Android and web together.
