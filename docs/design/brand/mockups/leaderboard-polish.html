What changes

Eight touches that finish the page

  1. A stage for the day's title. "Friday's Finest" sits on a spotlight: soft light rays and a glow in the host's color (pink for Friday's O), faint floating letter tiles and a few twinkling sparkles. The title floats gently. Each day uses its host's color (Monday blue for D, and so on).
  2. The date row becomes the stage's footer, tinted to match, so the card reads as one piece.
  3. Game picker: the selected game gets a filled purple tile instead of only a border, so it's obvious which board you're looking at.
  4. Play card: the big purple blob with an eye becomes a "View board" button, and the card gets the same colored top bar as the Home game cards. The W on the CLASSIC title is the happy one now.
  5. One result card. "Completed today" and "Your rank" merge into a single card: crown, your rank, how you solved it and your points, in the soft numbers.
  6. A podium for the top three, with the letter-tile avatars on gold, silver and bronze steps and a crown on first place. Everyone else lists below in one tidy card.
  7. Less white. Every card takes a soft wash of its own accent color (lavender for the Classic card, warm gold for your result, cream for the board, with soft striped rows) instead of plain white. The same rule applies to the cards on every menu.
  8. The rest of the kit: soft 3D controls with no bubbles, the full-width cast header, and the tab bar docked to the bottom with a warm tint to match this page.
