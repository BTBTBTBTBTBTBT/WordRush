Finished game

The result screen, finished

  1. No more text links. "Home" is gone (the house at the top already does that) and "Share" becomes the 3D share icon.
  2. The result line becomes two soft pills: 4 guesses and 0:48 in the soft numbers with 3D icons, instead of grey and purple text.
  3. Empty rows left after a win show as frosted glass tiles, not bright white.
  4. Score breakdown sits on a lavender card with a purple top bar, dashed dividers and purple values; the total is a big soft number.
  5. Today's word is spelled in purple tiles on a soft green card, with a green NOUN chip and the definition.
  6. The three buttons become chunky 3D buttons with icons (gold for the next daily, purple for the leaderboard, soft for Unlimited) that squish when pressed.

Streak and shield popups

Little celebrations, not plain bubbles

  1. Each popup gets a colored header (warm orange for the streak, purple for shields) with the big 3D icon, a friendly headline and a character: S the speedster cheers your streak, U the zen one guards your shields.
  2. The numbers sit in two soft tiles (current and best) in the soft rounded style.
  3. The streak popup shows this week as seven little day tiles, filled orange for each day played.
  4. The shield popup shows your shields as a row of 3D shields (the next one to earn is faded).
  5. The page behind dims softly, and the popup sits just under the button you tapped.

Footer pages

Seven pages, one family

  1. Every footer page gets its own title (How to Play, Guides, Strategy, Words, FAQ, Privacy, Terms), all the same height and style, each with the cast in a different pose mix. Guides no longer shows the How to Play title.
  2. Same layout on all seven: soft 3D back and help icons, the title, an intro card, then tinted cards with top bars (game guides use each game's own color).
  3. FAQ becomes question cards, and Privacy and Terms become tidy section cards with the same treatment, so even the legal pages feel finished.
