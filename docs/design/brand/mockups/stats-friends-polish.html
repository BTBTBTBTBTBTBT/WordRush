Stats · what changes

A finished, colorful dashboard

  1. The STATS title gets a stage: D's blue, with a faint notebook grid behind a soft glow, matching Monday's look since D is the numbers host.
  2. Your player card takes a soft lavender wash and a purple-to-pink top bar; the level bar uses the same gradient.
  3. The game rail becomes chips with each game's icon; the selected one is filled purple.
  4. Today sits in a soft blue card with the 8 game tiles and three white pills in the soft numbers.
  5. Streak and best moment become two tinted tiles with 3D icons; the all-time numbers become four tiles, each with its own color (purple wins, green win rate, gold win streak, pink daily streak).
  6. Charts sit on tinted cards with the purple bars, and every big number uses the soft rounded style.

Friends · what changes

Warm, social and full of color

  1. The FRIENDS title gets a stage: a soft pink-to-peach glow with little floating dots, like a party.
  2. The race banner keeps O cheering on the right, with a pink wash and a pink-to-gold top bar; the race chips use medal colors and soft numbers.
  3. Online friends show as letter-tile avatars with a green dot.
  4. Play with friends: each of the six games becomes a small card tinted in its own color with its own top bar, so the grid reads as colorful instead of white.
  5. This week's race gets the same podium as the Leaderboard, on a warm gold card.
  6. Tab bar: each tab squishes when you tap it, the same spongy press as the header icons (tap them to try it).
  7. Your friends list sits on a lavender card with soft striped rows and chunky Play / Challenge / Nudge buttons.
