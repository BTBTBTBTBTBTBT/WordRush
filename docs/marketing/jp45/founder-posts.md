## LinkedIn launch story (Fri Oct 16)

Ten weeks ago my business partner Brian Terchin and I had one daily word game and a group chat that never stopped.

The game existed for one reason: our families wanted to trash-talk each other over the same board every morning. It was never meant to be a company.

Then we kept building. Today we are launching Wordocious: 18 daily games with one score. Eight word games, from a classic six-guess board to eight boards at once. Ten puzzles, from number logic to a themed word search. One account, one streak, one leaderboard. Live head-to-head, so the daily puzzle is something you play against a person. A bot ladder with ten characters, where the last one has not been beaten yet. And a cast of ten letter characters who dress for the season. Halloween goes live tomorrow.

What I have learned in ten weeks, in three lines:
- A game people play against their family is the best test of whether it is any good.
- Small teams can ship a lot if every feature has to survive the family group chat.
- Free to play with a small optional Pro tier only works if the free version is the real product.

It is free on the web, iPhone and Android: https://wordocious.com/go/linkedin

If you play it and have a score to brag about, send it to me. Your move.
