## Maker comment (post first, as a maker comment)

Hi Product Hunt, I am Jasson, one of the two people behind Wordocious.

It started at a family table. We built one daily word game so our family could trash-talk each other over the same board, and then it got out of hand. Ten weeks later it is 18 daily games with one score: eight word games (Classic through Gauntlet) and ten puzzles, from Sudocious and Muddle to Kindred and Starsweep.

Three things we care about:
1. One streak, one score. Every game feeds the same leaderboard, so there is a reason to play all 18.
2. You play it against someone. Race a friend on the same puzzle, or take on the bot ladder, from Rip, who is a pushover, to Webster, who is not. Nobody has cleared it yet.
3. A cast, not a grid of gray squares. Ten letter characters with faces and costumes, and the whole app dresses for the season. This week it is Halloween.

It is free on web, iPhone and Android, and the daily puzzles stay free. We would love your honest feedback, especially on which of the 18 you reach for first. I will be here all day.
