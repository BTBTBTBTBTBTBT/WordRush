## What it is

Wordocious is a free daily puzzle app with 18 daily games, one score and a live head-to-head mode, hosted by a cast of ten letter characters. It plays the same on the web, iPhone and Android, and everyone gets the same puzzles each day, so friends and families can compare and trash-talk.

## The 18 daily games

Eight word dailies: Classic, Six, Seven, QuadWord, OctoWord, Succession, Deliverance and Gauntlet.

Ten puzzles: ProperNoundle, Sudocious, Muddle, Hubbub, Crosswordocious, Kindred, Letter Ladder, Codebreaker, Spyglass and Starsweep.

## What makes it different

- One account, one streak, one score across all 18 games, with global and friends leaderboards for each.
- Live VS: race a friend on the same puzzle, or climb the bot ladder against the ten-character cast, from the first rung (Rip) to the boss (Webster).
- An original cast: ten letter characters with faces, voices and costumes, plus a mascot builder with hats, colors and backdrops.
- Spoiler-free share cards for every game, so a result can be posted without giving away the answer.
- The whole app dresses for the season. Halloween runs Oct 17 to Oct 31; a Thanksgiving build follows in November.

## Free, and what Pro adds

Every daily is free to play. Wordocious Pro is optional: $6.99 a month, $59.99 a year, or a $1 day pass. Pro adds unlimited puzzles in every game, VS in every mode, streak shields, extra mascot items and deeper stats.

## The family origin

We built one daily word game so our family could trash-talk each other over the same board. Then it got out of hand. Two founders, Brian Terchin and Jasson Patterson, took it from one daily to 18 games, live head-to-head and a full cast in about ten weeks.
