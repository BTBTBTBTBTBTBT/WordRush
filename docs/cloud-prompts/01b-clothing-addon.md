ADDITION — clothing that conforms to the body (shirts, jerseys, hoodies, pants/shorts, skirts, dresses). Add this to the same branch and report, AFTER the core landmark work is done:

1. New garment rule type, "garment". A garment is shaped BY the body, not pinned to a point on it:
   - Tops (t-shirt, jersey, tank, hoodie, sweater): the body silhouette clipped between the shoulder line and the waist/hip line. It follows the outline exactly, with a hair of inset so it reads as worn. Sleeves are separate pieces fitted to each arm region (short, long, or none for tanks). The arms and hands stay on top where they cross the torso.
   - Bottoms (pants, shorts, skirt): the silhouette from the waist line down to just above the feet. Leg openings come from the feet/floor landmarks (shorts end higher). A skirt flares slightly past the hip width.
   - Dresses and overalls: top + bottom zones joined.
   - Clip each garment to the body outline (never outside it, except a skirt flare and a hood).
   - Shade it with the body's own lighting (top highlight, darker toward the edges and bottom) so it reads as fabric on a round body, plus a soft hem shadow.
2. Two-color garments for team jerseys: a primary zone and a secondary/trim zone (collar, sleeve bands, side panels, hem stripes), plus an optional third accent. Templates as zone maps:
   - basketball tank: wide arm holes, side panels
   - baseball button-front: pinstripe option
   - football: shoulder pads and a big number
   - hockey: hem stripes and a lace collar
   - soccer: V-neck and sleeve band
   Prototype the zones procedurally (flat zone fills plus the body lighting) to prove the fit. The final art comes later from ChatGPT.
3. The letter on clothing: when a top is worn, the mascot's letter is drawn ON the garment, as the jersey number or a chest print. It keeps the measured letter box and takes the garment's lighting. Guards keep it fully visible (no arm or held item may cover more than today's limit).
4. Guards for garments:
   - never past the outline (except allowed flares);
   - never over the face;
   - both shoulders covered symmetrically;
   - sleeves follow the arms;
   - no gap between top and bottom when both are worn (they overlap at the waist).
5. Contact sheets of a test t-shirt, test jersey (two colors), test hoodie, test shorts, test pants and a test skirt on every body and size variant, plus a jersey + pants combo on all of them. Look at every sheet.
6. Add "How to add clothing" to INTEGRATION.md and a garments section to REPORT-LANDMARKS.md.
