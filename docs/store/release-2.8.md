# Wordocious 2.8 — release notes and review copy (draft, 2026-10-10)

## What's New (App Store + Play, ≤ 4000 / 500 chars)

Happy Halloween from Wordocious!
• A spooky new look for the season: costumed cast, moonlit walls, bats and a witch on the wing.
• Everything is easier to read on the night theme: names, scores, labels and menus were reworked for contrast.
• New podiums and Leaderboard stage, with each player's own colors.
• Friends: pick-a-game dropdowns and a tidy resign flag on every pocket game.
• Rock Paper Scissors gets a SHOOT! countdown, and Call It has a real coin flip.
• Redesigned widgets in every size, starring your own mascot.
• Smoother screens, swipe right to close any game, and lots of polish.

## Expedited review request (App Store Connect → Contact Us → Request an Expedited App Review)

Reason: critical bug fix — readability.

Wordocious 2.7.1 (live) switches every screen to its Halloween night theme automatically on October 17. On that dark
theme several screens become hard or impossible to read: player names on the leaderboard podium render light-on-light,
How to Play text sits directly on the illustrated background, and buttons lose contrast (low-contrast brown on dark).
Version 2.8 fixes these: every section sits on its own card, names and labels are redrawn with contrast-tested colors
(our automated contrast tests pass for every Halloween surface), and button tints were corrected. We'd like 2.8 live
before October 17 so players never see the unreadable version. Thank you!
