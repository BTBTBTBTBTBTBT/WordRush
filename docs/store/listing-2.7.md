## Full description (both stores, ≤4000)

Wordocious is a whole world of daily word games, hosted by the WORDOCIOUS cast: ten chubby, glossy letter
characters who cheer you on, hand out trophies, and battle you in VS. Play 19 fresh puzzles every day, build
your own mascot, and race your friends to the top of the leaderboard.

WORDOCIOUS DAILIES
• Classic: one word, six tries
• Six and Seven: longer words with more tries
• QuadWord: four words at once
• OctoWord: eight boards, thirteen tries
• Succession: four words, one by one
• Deliverance: rescue four prefilled boards
• Gauntlet: five escalating stages in one run
Sweep every daily for a Daily Sweep, or solve them all with no wasted guess for a Flawless Victory.

PUZZLES
• ProperNoundle: guess the famous name
• Sudocious: daily number logic
• Muddle: unscramble the punchline
• Hubbub: seven letters, one hub
• Crosswordocious: a fill-in sayings crossword
• Kindred: find four groups of four
• Letter Ladder: change one letter at a time
• Codebreaker: crack the coded saying
• Spyglass: a themed word search
• Starsweep: one star per color region

VS BATTLES
• Race a friend on the same puzzle, live or on your own time
• Climb the bot ladder against all ten of the cast, from sleepy R to the mighty W
• Face the Bot of the Day and today's shared battle
• Challenge friends to a race of your best run

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
• Today's race: see who's leading your friends right now
• Friend streaks, reactions, and moments from your crew
• Quick pocket games: Rock Paper Scissors, Tic-Tac-Tile, Call It, Pass the Puzzle, Ghost and Word Chain
• Gift a friend a free week of Pro

MAKE IT YOURS
• Build your own mascot: pick a body, colors, patterns, eyes, mouth, hats, extras and a backdrop. Your
  initial rides on its belly, and it shows up on every leaderboard
• Earn 3D achievement badges and climb from Bronze to Diamond level tiers
• Keep your daily streak alive, with streak shields for the days life gets busy
• Sounds and haptics on every tap, flip and win
• Seasonal costumes for the whole cast, like Halloween

COMPETE AND TRACK
• Global daily leaderboards for every game, plus all-time records
• Detailed stats for every mode, with a Sweep view
• Share your results as a picture, starring the cast
• Home screen widgets with your streak, puzzles solved, points and the countdown to tomorrow

WORDOCIOUS PRO (OPTIONAL SUBSCRIPTION)
Play unlimited puzzles in every game, ad-free, with VS in every mode, four streak shields each billing period,
Pro hats, frames and backdrops for your mascot, a crown for W, and deeper stats. Daily puzzles are always free
to play without any purchase.
• Wordocious Pro Monthly and Yearly renew automatically unless canceled in your subscription settings.
• A one-time 24-hour Day Pass is also available.

Free to play. Sign in to save your progress and compete. New puzzles every day. How many can you solve?
